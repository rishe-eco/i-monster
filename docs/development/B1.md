## Built

- **Server.** Hono on `@hono/node-server`. `server.ts` boots; `app.ts` assembles, with middleware in the order headers → language → ETag (D-8), and a marker where B8's record goes. Every environment variable is read once, in `lib/env.ts`: `PORT` (4100), `DB_PATH` (`.tmp/dev.db`), `SITE_TZ` (`Asia/Tehran`), `E2E_CLOCK`, `PRELAUNCH`, `NODE_ENV`. Boot refuses `E2E_CLOCK=1` with `NODE_ENV=production`, and a bad port or timezone.
- **Schema.** `migrations/001_init.sql` holds all of plan §6.3 with its CHECKs, the triggers `episode_last_link` and `episode_publish_needs_link`, and one more (*Decided*). `lib/db.ts` turns on WAL, foreign keys and a 5 s busy timeout on every connection, and runs numbered migrations at boot.
- **One-rule files.** `lib/clock.ts`, `lib/limits.ts` (all of §6.4, plus `backoffSeconds`), `lib/ids.ts` (PD-6), `lib/dates.ts` (`formatEntryDate`, `formatAdminDate`, `feedDate`, all from `formatToParts`), `lib/i18n.ts`, `lib/switch.ts` (§6.5 and the hreflang alternates), `lib/headers.ts` (PD-18, PD-22), `lib/assets.ts`.
- **Pages.** `/` and `/fa/` (landing), `/fa` → 308 → `/fa/`, and one designed 404 per language for any unknown path. Also `/healthz`, `robots.txt`, `/assets/*` with hashed names and a year of `immutable` caching, and `POST /__test/clock`, mounted only with `E2E_CLOCK=1`.
- **Shell.** CSS rebuilt from the design's tokens (D-6): `tokens.css`, `base.css`, `components.css`, `fonts.css`. Light and dark follow `prefers-color-scheme`. Logical properties only. `forced-colors` rules for `<mark>` and fields. Strings are in `locales/{en,fa}.ts` under one `Strings` type; landing content is in `content/site.{en,fa}.ts`, marked placeholder (P0-6).
- **Fonts.** Self-hosted woff2 with their OFL licences, from `@fontsource` by `npm pack` (D-1). Bricolage Grotesque 500–800 and Newsreader 400 ship Latin only. Estedad 500/700/800 and Vazirmatn 400 ship **both** their Arabic and Latin subsets (*Decided*). Each face's `unicode-range` is its exact cmap.
- **Harness.** `npm test` (Vitest), `npm run e2e` (Playwright, Edge, `light` and `dark` projects at 360 px), `npm run check` (`tsc --noEmit` and ESLint, which bans `new Date()` and `Date.now()` outside `lib/clock.ts`). There are 15 Vitest files, including a source scan for the house rules, and two e2e specs.
- **Repo.** `CLAUDE.md` (the ten rules of plan §0.3 word for word, the commands, the environment), `README.md` with the pointer to the module folder, `.gitignore`, `.env.example`, `.gitattributes` (review), and this folder's stage list.

- Both e2e specs gained a 200% check, in both languages and both schemes. `expectNoSidewaysScroll` now waits for the web fonts before it measures, because the narrower fallback faces hid the overflow. I confirmed the check fails on the unfixed CSS (218 px and 192 px against 180).
- `.gitattributes` pins LF, because `core.autocrlf` is on here (report item 5).
- `.env.example` no longer says to copy it to `.env`, since nothing loads that file. It now names `node --env-file=.env --import tsx server.ts`, which I tried and which works.

## Decided

The lane's calls (report item 4), each accepted at review:

- **Fonts: both subsets of each Persian family.** The Arabic subsets lack «», the space, `.`, `:`, `-`, `—` and ASCII digits, and the Latin subsets supply them. The card's stop rule was about whether the families can draw Persian, and they can. The lane read it correctly. PD-26's "Arabic subsets" is a plan defect (below).
- **An extra trigger, `episode_publish_needs_link_on_insert`.** An episode can't be inserted already published. **B7** creates the draft, adds a link, then publishes. RAISE messages are `LAST_LINK` and `LINK_REQUIRED`, the §6.2 codes.
- **Schema beyond §6.3:**
  - `auth_throttle` is one row, `CHECK (id = 1)`, inserted by the migration.
  - `episode.date` is `NOT NULL` with a `YYYY-MM-DD` GLOB. This agrees with PD-23 (the date defaults to today in `SITE_TZ`), but the code must supply it: **B7**.
  - The credential flags are 0/1, and `session.opened_by` and `device_credential` are `NOT NULL`.
  - The record tables are `WITHOUT ROWID`.
  - The episode content columns (`name`, `description`, `tag1`, `tag2`) are nullable, because a draft needs only a language (PD-23).
- **`SITE_ORIGIN = 'https://imnstr.com'`**, a constant in `lib/switch.ts`, for absolute hreflang URLs. No environment variable, because there is one origin.
- **Hashed assets** come from a startup manifest (sha256, 10 hex), with no build step. Unhashed and unknown asset paths get the designed 404, and licence files aren't served.
- **The rest.** `/fa` → 308 → `/fa/`. HSTS is two years, with no `includeSubDomains` or preload (founder's call, below). Pages get `no-cache` and an ETag on 200, `/admin*` gets `no-store`, and `/healthz` gets `no-store`. On phones the header nav drops Feed, as in the design's phone plates, and the footer keeps it. Arrows are inline SVG, mirrored in Persian. The favicon is `data:,`. `CLOCK_INVALID` is a test-only code, outside §6.2.

**Where the plan or card was wrong** (report item 5, answered). Items 1, 2, 4 and 5 go to the plan's changelog through the founder:
1. **PD-26 and plan §4:** "the Arabic subset" isn't enough. Ship the Arabic and Latin subsets of Estedad and Vazirmatn.
2. **§6.3:** "episode: like `entry`" is ambiguous. The columns are as in `001_init.sql`.
3. **hreflang** needs an absolute origin. Settled by `SITE_ORIGIN`.
4. **§5 B1, Logic** asks for "a 23:59 publish in Tehran, which is another day in UTC". That is wrong: 23:59 in Tehran is 20:29 UTC, the same day. The window where the dates differ is 00:00–03:29 in Tehran. The tests cover that real case (00:00 on 1 January in Tehran is 31 December in UTC) and also pin the 23:59 case. *(Found at review.)*
5. **Review checklist §3 and plan §3** say "`server.ts` gains one line" per stage. As built, routes are mounted in `app.ts`, and `CLAUDE.md` says so. *(Found at review.)*
6. **The card's 401-enumeration test** has nothing to enumerate until B4a, which creates it.
